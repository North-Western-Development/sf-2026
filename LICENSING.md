# Licensing

Subspace Frequency is **source-available**: free for people, communities and small businesses, with a paid license for larger businesses. Its SDKs are open source, so anyone can build on it.

| Part | License | Where |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| The platform: server, web, desktop and mobile apps, docs, deployment scripts and everything else not listed below | [PolyForm Small Business 1.0.0](LICENSE.md) | `LICENSE.md` |
| The SDKs | [MIT](https://opensource.org/licenses/MIT) | the `LICENSE` file in each |

The MIT-licensed SDKs:

- `packages/bot-sdk`: bots for JavaScript, TypeScript and Deno
- `packages/activity-sdk`: custom Activities
- `packages/rich-presence`: Rich Presence for JavaScript
- `sdk/`: the bot SDKs for Python, Java, C# and C++, and Rich Presence for C++, C#, Unity and Unreal Engine

## Who can use the platform for free

Under PolyForm Small Business you may use, change and share the platform for free if you are:

- an individual, for any purpose;
- a community, club or other group that is not a business, such as a gaming community, a hobby group or a study group;
- a charity, school, public research organization or government body;
- a business with **fewer than 100** employees and contractors **and less than US$1,000,000** in revenue in the prior tax year (in 2019 dollars, adjusted for inflation).

That includes running your own instance for your community, changing it, and sharing your changes under the same terms. [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) has the exact terms, and it, not this summary, is what applies.

## Commercial licenses

A business at or over either limit needs a commercial license to use the platform, including running it internally or for customers. Commercial licenses are also available for anything the free terms do not allow.

Contact **april.abney@northwesterndevelopment.com**, with your organization's name and how you plan to use Subspace Frequency.

The SDKs never need a commercial license: build and sell bots, Activities and games with them freely under MIT.
